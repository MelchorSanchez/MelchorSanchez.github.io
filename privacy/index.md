This is a personal website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or any kind of visitor tracking.

**Who is responsible.** Melchor Sanchez-Martinez. You can reach me at melchor .at. msanchezmartinez.com (replace ".at." with "@").

**Contact form.** If you write to me through the contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are sent through [Formspree](https://formspree.io), which delivers them to my inbox and may store them on servers outside the European Union. I use them only to answer you and keep them for as long as needed to deal with your message. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

**Comments.** Comments on blog posts are handled by [utterances](https://utteranc.es) and stored as issues in the site's public [GitHub repository](https://github.com/MelchorSanchez/MelchorSanchez.github.io). To comment you sign in with your GitHub account, and GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that.

**Hosting and third-party resources.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses as part of normal operation. Icon fonts are loaded from Font Awesome's servers, which also receive your IP address when a page loads.

**Your rights.** You can ask me for access to, correction of or deletion of your data by writing to the address above. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the [AEPD](https://www.aepd.es)).
